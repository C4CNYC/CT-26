Teachers, students and admins all sign in on the same page, at your platform's address, such as codetribe.ai.

## Sign in with Google

1. Open [codetribe.ai/auth/signIn](https://codetribe.ai/auth/signIn).
2. Select **Sign in with Google**.
3. Choose the Google account you signed up with.

![The sign-in page, with the Email box and the Sign in with Google button](/docs/getting-started/sign-in-and-reset-your-password-1-sign-in-page.png)

## Sign in with your email and password

1. On the sign-in page, type your email in **Email**, then select the arrow next to it, or press Enter.
2. Type your password in **Password**.
3. Select **LET'S GO!**

![The password step, with the Forgot password link and the LET'S GO! button](/docs/getting-started/sign-in-and-reset-your-password-2-password-step.png)

If you see **Email not found or wrong password**, select **GO BACK** and check both. If you don't have an account yet, sign up first.

## Reset a forgotten password

1. On the password step, select **Forgot password**.
2. Type the email address you signed up with in **Email Address**, then select **Send Request**.
3. Open the email and select its link. It opens the **Pick your new password:** page.
4. Type a new password in **Password**, and again in **Confirm New Password**, then select **Update Password**.
5. Sign in with your new password.

![The Forgot your password? page, with the Email Address box and the Send Request button](/docs/getting-started/sign-in-and-reset-your-password-3-forgot-password-page.png)

If the page says **Make sure you have a valid reset link**, the link doesn't work. Select **Go to reset password** and ask for a new email.
