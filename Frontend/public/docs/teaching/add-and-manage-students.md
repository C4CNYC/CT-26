Use this when your students can't sign up on their own. The **Students** page is in the dashboard. Admins open the dashboard from their account menu: select your picture at the top right, then **Manager Account**.

## Add a student

1. In the sidebar, select **Students**, then **Add Student**.

![The Invite Student and Add Student buttons on the Students page](/docs/teaching/add-and-manage-students-1-add-student-button.png)

2. Type the student's **Email Address**, **First Name** and **Last Name**. The other boxes are optional.
3. Copy the **Generated password** and keep it safe. The student signs in with their email and this password.
4. Select **Create User**. You see **Create success!**, and the student shows in the list.

![The new student form, with the generated password hidden](/docs/teaching/add-and-manage-students-2-new-student-form.png)

![The Students list, with three students](/docs/teaching/add-and-manage-students-3-student-list.png)

To put the student in a class, see [Add students who already have an account](/docs/teaching/invite-students-to-your-class#add-students-who-already-have-an-account).

## Edit a student

1. On the **Students** page, select **⋮** at the end of the student's row, then **Edit**.

![The menu at the end of a student's row, with Delete and Edit](/docs/teaching/add-and-manage-students-4-row-menu.png)

2. Change their details, then select **Save Changes**.

![A student's edit page, with their name and details](/docs/teaching/add-and-manage-students-5-edit-student.png)

## Give a student a new password

1. On the student's edit page, select **Change Password**. A **Generated password** box opens below it, with a new password.
2. Select the **Change Password** button under that box. The page shows the new password.
3. Give the new password to the student.

## Remove a student

On the **Students** page, select **⋮** at the end of the student's row, then **Delete**, and confirm.

This takes the student out of your school and its classes. If they aren't in another school, their account and their work are deleted too, and you can't undo it. To take a student out of one class only, use the class's [Members tab](/docs/teaching/invite-students-to-your-class#see-who-joined) instead.
