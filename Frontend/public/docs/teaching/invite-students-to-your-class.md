You invite students from your class's page. To open it, select your class in the sidebar, under **TRIBES**.

![A class page, with the Join-code at the top left and the Members tab](/docs/teaching/invite-students-to-your-class-1-class-page.png)

## Share the invite link

1. On your class page, select **Share**, at the top right of the class banner.
2. Select **Copy**, then send the link to your students, for example by email.

Students who open the link can join your class.

![The Share group box, with the invite link and the Copy button](/docs/teaching/invite-students-to-your-class-2-share-group.png)

## Give students the join code

1. On your class page, find **Join-code** at the top left, and select the copy icon next to it.
2. Give the code to your students. They type it on the **Join a tribe** page: in the sidebar, under **TRIBES**, **+**, then **Join a group**.

## Add students who already have an account

If your students already have accounts in your school, you can add them to the class yourself.

1. On your class page, select **•••** at the top right of the class banner, then **Edit**.
2. Select the **Students** tab, then **Add students**.
3. Select each student. Each one joins the class straight away.

![The Students tab of Class Preferences, with the school's students listed under Add students](/docs/teaching/invite-students-to-your-class-3-add-students-list.png)

4. Select **Close**.

![Three students added to the class](/docs/teaching/invite-students-to-your-class-4-students-added.png)

## See who joined

On your class page, select the **Members** tab. It lists everyone in the class.

![The Members tab, with three students](/docs/teaching/invite-students-to-your-class-5-members-tab.png)

To take a student out of the class, select **⋮** at the end of their row, then **Delete**, and confirm. This only removes them from this class.
