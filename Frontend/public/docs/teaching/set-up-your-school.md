Your school's details show in the **About** box on the School page.

The School page is in the dashboard. Admins open the dashboard from their account menu: select your picture at the top right, then **Manager Account**.

## Edit your school's details

1. If you have more than one school, choose the school in the school menu at the top right.
2. In the sidebar, select **School**.
3. On the **General** tab, fill in **Name**, **Country**, **Address** and **City**.
4. Optionally, type a phone number in the box with the flag, and a short description in **About**.
5. Select **Save Changes**. You see **Update info success!**

![The General tab of the School page, with the Name, Country, Address and City boxes](/docs/teaching/set-up-your-school-1-general-tab.png)

Your details now show in the **About** box on the left.
