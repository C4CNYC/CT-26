On CodeTribe, an assignment is called a Quest. Your class's Quests show on the class page.

## Browse the courses

1. In the sidebar, select **Catalog**. It lists all the official courses.
2. On a course, select **Explore** to see its modules.

![A course in the Catalog, with the Explore button](/docs/teaching/find-a-course-and-assign-it-to-a-class-1-course-card.png)

## Assign a course or a module

1. Open your class: in the sidebar, under **TRIBES**, select it.
2. On the **Quests** tab, select **Add Quest**, then **Assign from Catalog**.

![The Add Quest menu, with Assign from Catalog](/docs/teaching/find-a-course-and-assign-it-to-a-class-2-add-quest-menu.png)

3. Under **Select a course**, choose the course.
4. Under **Select module**, choose one module, or leave **All modules** to assign the whole course.
5. Optionally, change the **Quest name**. CodeTribe suggests one for you.

![The Details tab of Add Quest, with a course, a module and the Quest name](/docs/teaching/find-a-course-and-assign-it-to-a-class-3-add-quest-details.png)

6. Optionally, select the **Settings** tab and change the **Due Date**. Keep **Status** at **Published**.

![The Settings tab of Add Quest, with the dates and the status](/docs/teaching/find-a-course-and-assign-it-to-a-class-4-add-quest-settings.png)

7. Select **Launch**. The Quest shows on the class's **Quests** tab, with its due date.

![The new Quest on the Quests tab, with its due date](/docs/teaching/find-a-course-and-assign-it-to-a-class-5-quest-card.png)
